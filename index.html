Information We Collect

We collect information in order to provide and improve our services. The types of information we collect include:

1.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you register for an account, we may collect your name, email address, username, and password.
  • Profile Information: Any additional information you choose to add to your profile, such as a profile photo or bio.
  • Communications: Information you provide when you contact us for support or send us feedback.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the App, including features accessed, time spent, and actions taken.
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, and referring/exit pages.
  • Location Information: General location based on your IP address or precise location if you grant permission.

Note: We only collect precise location data with your explicit consent, which you can revoke at any time through your device settings.

Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest;

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
